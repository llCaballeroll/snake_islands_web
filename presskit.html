About the game

The classic Snake, now on floating islands.

Snake Islands reinvents the classic snake game for phones: you steer with one finger, play in short runs, and every point makes the island faster and more dangerous.

Besides eating eggs to grow, you dodge bombs that drop every 10 seconds and explode in a 3×3 area, and the Skull, a ghost that appears from level 4. The golden egg (15% chance) shrinks the snake and fills an energy bar to move in turbo or slow motion.

Your best score unlocks 10 islands, from "Level 1" to "IMPOSSIBLE" (10,000 points), and 77 skins for the snake, the eggs, the bombs, the Skull and the background. It has global leaderboards on Game Center and Google Play Games, and a card to challenge friends from any app.

Key points

  • One finger, short runs: learn it in seconds.
  • 10 islands with rising speed and danger.
  • 77 skins, earned by score or in boxes with published odds: 5 fragments per box, same chance for every skin.
  • Global leaderboards and achievements on Game Center and Google Play Games.
  • No sign-up and available in 16 languages.
  • Built with Flutter and Flame by a single Mexican developer.
