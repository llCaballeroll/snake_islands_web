Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 21, 2026

Leer en español

In short

  • No account needed. We never ask for your name, email or phone number.
  • Your progress (score, skins, diamonds and settings) is saved only on your phone. We have no servers with player data.
  • Ads are shown by Google AdMob. In the European Union, the United Kingdom and Switzerland we ask for your consent first, and in some US states you can ask that your data not be sold or shared.
  • Purchases are processed by Apple or Google. We never see your payment details.

1. Who is responsible

Snake Islands is an independent game developed by Luis Angel Caballero Jardón, based in Metepec, State of Mexico, Mexico, who is responsible for processing the data described in this policy. For any privacy matter, write to soporte.snakeislands@outlook.com.

On iPhone, the app does not ask for tracking permission (App Tracking Transparency), so it does not access Apple's advertising identifier (IDFA). The add to Photos permission is only used if you choose to save the image of your score.

3. What we don't do

  • We don't sell your personal data.
  • We don't ask for your location, contacts, camera or microphone.
  • We don't read your photos: we can only add the image you choose to save.
  • We don't build profiles of you or share your progress with third parties.

4. Advertising and consent

The free version shows ads from Google AdMob: an ad when the app opens, a banner, and rewarded ads that you choose to watch. Google may use cookies or device identifiers to show ads and measure their performance. You can read how in How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services.

European Union, United Kingdom and Switzerland

The first time you open the game you'll see a Google consent form compatible with the IAB TCF framework. You can accept, reject or choose which purposes you allow. If you don't accept, ads will be non-personalized.

United States

If you live in a state with a privacy law (for example, California), you'll see an option to ask that your data not be sold or shared for advertising.

How to limit personalized ads

  • Android: Settings › Google › Ads › Delete advertising ID.
  • iPhone: Settings › Privacy & Security › Apple Advertising.
  • You can also remove all ads with the one-time "Remove ads" purchase.

5. In-app purchases

Box packs, golden eggs and "Remove ads" are paid through the App Store or Google Play. They process the payment under their own privacy policy. We only receive confirmation that the purchase was valid so we can deliver it. We never receive your card number or bank details.

Data involved in a purchase: the email address of your Apple or Google account that the store uses to process the purchase; the app does not store it.

6. Children

Snake Islands is suitable for all ages, but it is not specifically directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children. If you are a parent or guardian and believe your child gave us personal information, write to us and we will delete it. We recommend turning on your device's parental controls for purchases.

7. Retention and deletion

  • Game progress: stays on your phone until you uninstall the app or delete its data, which removes it completely.
  • Ad, purchase and leaderboard data: kept by Google, Apple and their services under their own policies. You can manage it from your Google or Apple account.

8. Your rights

You can ask to access, correct or delete your data or object to its use (ARCO rights, under Mexico's Federal Law on the Protection of Personal Data Held by Private Parties), and you can withdraw your consent. If you live in the European Union or the United Kingdom you also have your rights under the GDPR, and in California those under the CCPA/CPRA.

Because we don't keep player data on our own servers, the fastest way is usually to delete the app's data or use Google's and Apple's privacy tools. If you need anything else, write to soporte.snakeislands@outlook.com with the subject "Privacy". We reply within 20 business days at most.

9. Security and transfers

All of the app's communication with Apple, Google and AdMob is encrypted (HTTPS/TLS). These providers may process data on servers outside Mexico, mainly in the United States, with the safeguards the law requires.

10. Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we'll update the date above and, if the change is significant, we'll let you know in the app or on the store listing.
