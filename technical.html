Engineering

How it's made

One Dart codebase for iPhone, iPad and Android: Flutter for the interface and Flame for the game.

Flutter 3.47 Dart 3.10 Flame 1.34 iOS + Android v1.1.3

The foundation

Game engine

Flame runs the game loop on a grid: the snake, the food, the bombs, the Skull and the explosions are independent components.

Interface

Flutter draws the menus, the shop and the settings with a glass effect, dark mode and animations that adapt to 60 and 120 Hz screens.

Monetization

Google Mobile Ads (app open, banner and rewarded) with UMP consent, and in-app purchases with StoreKit and Google Play Billing 8.

Social

Leaderboards and achievements with Game Center and Google Play Games, and a share card for your high score that is generated on the phone itself.

Project structure

The game logic (Flame) lives apart from the interface (Flutter), and external services are isolated in their own folder.

snake_game/lib
lib/
├── main.dart                 // startup, languages, theme and global audio
├── game/
│   ├── snake_game.dart       // game loop, levels and rules
│   ├── components/           // snake, food, bomb, ghost, explosion
│   └── managers/             // asset loading, milestones and skins
├── screens/                  // home, levels, game, shops, settings, onboarding
├── services/                 // ads, purchases, menu music, sharing
├── widgets/                  // liquid glass, transitions, haptics
├── theme/                    // light/dark colors
└── l10n/                     // text in 16 languages

Technical decisions

Performance

  • Assets preloaded before each run so there's no stutter.
  • RepaintBoundary around the shop's endless animations so they don't repaint the whole screen.
  • High refresh rate on Android with flutter_displaymode.
  • Optimized images and sounds; the menu music is cut to loop without a seam.

Reliability

  • No duplicate purchases: every transaction is delivered once and always closed with the store.
  • Respectful audio: the music obeys the iPhone's silent switch and pauses in the background.
  • Build flags (--dart-define) for test ads and debugging that never reach production.
  • Privacy consent (UMP) before requesting ads.

Publishing

iOS

Built with Xcode, automatic signing and uploaded to TestFlight with a script. Rated 9+.

Android

App Bundle signed with an upload key and Google Play App Signing, with Play Billing Library 8.

One version

The same version and build number ship on both stores, tested first on iPhone and Android.

app-ads.txt

Published on the developer's domain so AdMob can verify the ad inventory.
