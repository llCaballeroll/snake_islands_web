How to play

Easy to learn.
Hard to put down.

One finger, four directions and an island that gets faster and more dangerous with every point.

  • Eat and grow

    Swipe to change direction. Every egg adds a point and makes your snake longer. Don't bite yourself.

    +1 pointSwipe ↑ ↓ ← →

  • Bombs

    One drops every 10 seconds. Once it lands you have 4 seconds before it blows up a 3×3 area.

    Head: game overBody: −50% points

  • The Skull

    From level 4 on, a ghost roams the island waiting for your mistake. Keep your head away from it.

    Head: game overBody: −1 point/sec

  • Golden egg

    Shows up 15% of the time. It shrinks your snake to 3 segments and fills your energy: hold to go turbo or slow motion.

    MiracleEnergy 100%
